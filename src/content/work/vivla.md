Vivla is an innovative real estate company offering a co-ownership model for holiday homes. It allows clients to purchase shares in premium properties, starting from 1/8 of a home, you own it and have the freedom to sell when desired. Vivla handles all maintenance, giving homeowners a hassle-free experience and access to our network of properties across top destinations. The company’s mission is to make second home ownership more accessible, affordable, and enjoyable, offering flexibility and hassle-free holiday experiences.

# Jorge Orejas — Personal Website Design Canon

## Core Definition

**jorgeorejas.com is a logbook.**

Not a portfolio.  
Not a personal brand.  
Not a startup landing page.

It is the written trace of a vivid, ambitious mind under pressure.
Ideas arrive fast. Structure keeps them legible.
Enjoyment lives in the margins.

**A year lived awake, strong, ambitious — and enjoyed.**

The website must breathe this quietly.

---

## Design Spine

**Writing machine first. Personality second.**

- Ideas > person
- Notes > essays
- Judgment > explanation
- Structure > decoration

If something looks good but slows reading, it is wrong.

---

## Typography System (Google Fonts Only)

### 1. Display Typeface — Intensity & Direction

**Font:** `Bricolage Grotesque `
```
Optical Size: Auto
Width: 75
Weight: 700
```
**Source:** Google Fonts

Role:
- Page titles
- Section headers
- Structural anchors

Why:
- Condensed, vertical, unapologetic
- Feels ambitious and directional
- No personality noise — just force

Rules:
- Uppercase only
- Used sparingly
- Never used for body text
- Letter-spacing: +0.04em to +0.06em

This font supplies **ambition and pressure**.

---

### 2. Body Typeface — The Writing Machine

**Font:** `IBM Plex Sans`  
**Source:** Google Fonts

Role:
- Notes
- Essays
- Thinking
- Lists
- Everything that must be read

Why:
- Neutral but not bland
- Slightly technical
- Feels like a tool, not a voice
- Excellent at small sizes

Weights:
- Regular (400)
- Medium (500)

Rules:
- Short line length
- Tight but breathable line-height
- No typographic tricks

This font is the **engine**.

---

### 3. Handwritten Accent Typeface — Human Trace

**Font:** `Caveat`  
**Source:** Google Fonts

Role:
- Micro annotations
- Marginal notes
- Asides
- Occasional emphasis

Why:
- Feels personal, not childish
- Reads like a note added after the fact
- Signals “this passed through a human”

Rules:
- Very small usage
- Never for long text
- Never decorative-only
- Used like handwriting in a lab notebook

This font is **enjoyment**.

## Color System (Locked)

### Core Colors

```txt
Ink Black        #0B0B0B
Paper White     #F7F6F3
Soft Gray       #8A8A8A
````

Usage:

* Ink Black → primary text
* Paper White → dominant background
* Soft Gray → secondary metadata

Contrast:

* High, but softened
* No pure white / pure black

---

### Accent Colors

#### Primary Accent — Editorial Mark

```txt
Orange Ink      #FF4F00
```

Feels like:

* Correction
* Attention
* Editorial emphasis

Usage:

* Dates
* Categories
* Inline emphasis
* Small dividers
* Status markers

Never:

* Large surfaces
* Buttons
* Headline fills

---

#### Secondary Accent — Blue Ballpoint

```txt
Ballpoint Blue  #3300FF
```

Feels like:

* Notes in the margin
* Afterthoughts
* Human interference

Usage:

* Handwritten annotations
* Micro-details
* Rare emphasis

This color must feel **accidental but intentional**.

---

## Layout Principles

### Vertical Rhythm

* Content is stacked, not boxed
* Clear separation between sections
* Tight grouping inside sections

Hierarchy is created by:

* Spacing
* Scale
* Alignment

Never by color noise.

---

### Grid Discipline

* Single column by default
* Multi-column only when structurally required
* No cards
* No dashboards
* No visual containers

This is a notebook, not an interface.

---

## Content Structure

### Homepage

Acts as:

* Cover
* Index
* Entry point to thought

Contains:

* One strong opening statement (short, declarative)
* Clear sections:

  * Notes
  * Essays
  * Projects
  * Ideas in progress

No hero CTA.
No pitch.
Your name appears quietly.

---

### Pages

Each page should feel like:

* A working document
* A thought captured before it was safe
* Something written to think, not to perform

Prefer:

* Fewer pieces
* Stronger signal
* Slow accumulation

---

## Micro-Enjoyment (Critical)

Enjoyment does NOT come from:

* Animations
* Visual tricks
* Clever layouts

Enjoyment lives in:

* Marginal notes (handwritten font)
* Unexpected line breaks
* Small orange marks
* The sense of a mind thinking on paper

This is where the site smiles — briefly.

---

## Motion & Interaction

* Minimal
* Functional
* Near invisible

No parallax.
No scroll theatre.
Nothing that competes with reading.

---

## Absolute Red Lines

This site must never feel:

* Startupy
* Branded
* Influencer-like
* Over-designed
* Polished for approval

If it starts to feel impressive instead of precise, it has failed.

---

## Final Sentence (Protect This)

**jorgeorejas.com is the logbook of a vivid mind whose ideas brim faster than they can be polished.**

It is:

* Awake in attention
* Strong in structure
* Ambitious in direction
* Enjoyed in the margins
